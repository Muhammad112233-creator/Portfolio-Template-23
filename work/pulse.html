Pulse

A streaming player designed for listeners, not feeds.

Role
Product Designer
Year
2025
Sector
Music Player Redesign
Stack
UX · UI · Interaction · Prototyping

Overview

Pulse is a self-initiated redesign concept for a mainstream streaming app. The premise: music players have become social networks with a play button. Pulse strips the interface back to the song — a now-playing screen that reacts to the live waveform, so every track gets its own light show.

The Challenge

The core constraint was attention. Every added element steals from the moment of listening. The design had to be navigable with one thumb, one eye on the road, zero reading.

The Process

01

Prototype the feeling first

Before any wireframes, I built a WebAudio prototype where visuals respond to frequency bands. If it felt magical on a phone speaker, the concept survived.

02

Delete ruthlessly

Six screens became three. Search, queue and library collapsed into one continuous sheet with progressive disclosure.

03

Stress-test the edge cases

Lock screen, CarPlay, tiny Android screens, and the dreaded 'accidentally tapped while jogging' scenario all got dedicated passes.

The Outcome

The concept case study became my most-shared work — featured in two design newsletters and forked by teams exploring audio-reactive UI. It directly led to three client engagements about music products.

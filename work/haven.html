Overview

Haven is a property platform built around one observation: people do not search for three bedrooms — they search for morning sun and a short walk to the water. As front-end lead I built the map-first browsing experience and the natural-language search behind it.

The Challenge

Property search is a filter-form monoculture: dropdowns, sliders, despair. We needed to index subjective qualities — light, quiet, vibe — without inventing fake precision, and render 30,000 listings on a map without melting phones.

The Process

01

Cluster with care

A custom clustering layer keeps the map readable at every zoom, with animations choreographed so markers never teleport.

02

Honest search

Natural-language queries like “sunny balcony near the water” translate into weighted, explainable filters — users see exactly why a home matched.

03

Performance as feature

List virtualization, image budgets, and a hard 200KB JS ceiling. The fastest property site in its market was a design decision.

The Outcome

Haven launched as the second most-visited property platform in its market within a year. Search-to-listing click-through doubled versus the incumbent pattern library it replaced.

Overview

A Bergen roastery with cult status at the farmers market wanted an online store with the same warmth. As creative developer I built a scroll-driven storefront where each roast tells its story — altitude, process, tasting notes — rendered as a slow cinematic scroll ending in a one-minute checkout.

The Challenge

Selling coffee online means selling the one thing screens cannot transmit: aroma. The experience needed to slow visitors down enough to care — and then get out of the way completely at purchase time.

The Process

01

Story per bean

Each roast got a scrollytelling chapter built on lightweight WebGL: steam particles, altitude maps, and process illustrations that respond to scroll velocity.

02

Brew-guide generator

A small tool that turns your equipment and grind into a printable recipe card. It became the most shared page on the site.

03

Sixty-second checkout

Guest-first checkout, digital wallets, and a three-tap subscription flow. No account walls, no newsletter hostage-taking.

The Outcome

Conversion tripled against the old shop. Average session time rose to 3m 40s, and the brew-guide generator alone now drives a fifth of all organic traffic.

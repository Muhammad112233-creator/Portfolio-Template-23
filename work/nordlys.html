Nordlys

A mobile bank that treats your money like weather — always visible, never stormy.

Overview

Nordlys is a Norwegian challenger bank with one radical belief: banking apps do not need to feel like tax forms. I led product design from the first workshop to the App Store launch, building a design language where balances “breathe” and insights arrive like the northern lights — quietly, and exactly when you look up.

The Challenge

Money is anxiety for most people. The existing app buried balance information behind three tabs, used red as its primary accent, and explained fees in footnotes. We needed to rebuild trust in an interface people open five times a day — without losing the density power users demanded.

The Process

01

Listen before drawing

We ran 24 diary studies and mapped every moment users felt stressed about money. The finding: people check balances to feel safe, not to do math.

02

Design the glance

We prototyped a home screen readable in under two seconds — one number, one trend, one suggested action. Everything else lives one intentional layer deeper.

03

Ship, measure, tune

We launched behind a feature flag cohort, instrumented time-to-balance, and iterated motion curves until the app felt calm instead of slow.

The Outcome

The redesign shipped to 400,000 users. Time-to-balance dropped from 8.2s to 1.4s, support tickets about “where did my money go” fell by a third, and the app reached a 4.8 App Store rating — the highest in its category in Norway.
